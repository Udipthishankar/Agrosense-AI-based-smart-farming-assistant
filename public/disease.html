Plant Condition References

This version provides educational examples only. It does not identify plant conditions from uploaded images. For a real diagnosis or treatment decision, consult a qualified local agricultural specialist.

  • Leaf rust: orange-brown pustules can occur on leaf surfaces.
  • Powdery mildew: white powder-like growth may appear on leaves and stems.
  • Early or late blight: leaf spots or dark lesions can have several possible causes.
  • Bacterial wilt: sudden wilting requires professional assessment.
